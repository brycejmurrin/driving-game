HOW TO PLAY

STEERING

TILT Tilt your phone left/right — gyro calibrates at countdown

TOUCH Touch-hold left/right half of screen when tilt is off

BUTTONS ◀ ▶ on-screen arrows (shown when tilt off)

DRIFT & BOOST

DRIFT Hold the DRIFT button (or Space) through a corner

Sparks turn blue → release for a big speed boost

Drift hop also clears cones and oil slicks
