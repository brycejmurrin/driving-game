HOW TO PLAY

📱 iOS / MOBILE

STEERING — TILT ON (default)

Tilt your phone left/right to steer — gyro calibrates at the countdown

iOS will ask motion permission on your first tap — allow it

If neutral drifts, tap PAUSE then RECALIBRATE TILT

STEERING — TILT OFF

◀ ▶ Arrow buttons appear at bottom-left and bottom-right

Toggle tilt off/on in the pause menu → TILT

DRIFT & BOOST — iOS

DRIFT Hold the DRIFT button (bottom-right) through a corner

Sparks turn blue → release = big speed boost

A quick hop also clears cones and oil slicks

WEAPONS — iOS

Drive through a ? box to collect a weapon

Tilt on: weapon button appears bottom-left — tap to fire

Tilt off: weapon button appears center-bottom — tap between the arrows

🚀 Missile — homes in on the kart ahead, spins it out

⚡ Boost — instant speed burst

💧 Oil — drops a slick behind you

PAUSE & SOUND — iOS

❚❚ Pause button top-right during a race

♪ Sound toggle top-right on menu and select screens

💻 COMPUTER / DESKTOP

CONTROLS — COMPUTER

← → or A D Steer

Space Hold to drift — release for boost

X Fire weapon — or click the FIRE button on screen

↓ or S Brake

P or Esc Pause

WEAPON BUTTON — COMPUTER

The FIRE button is always visible bottom-left during a race

It lights up with the weapon icon when you collect a ? box

Click it or press X to fire
